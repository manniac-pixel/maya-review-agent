## Screen-Level Mapping

### 1. ONBOARDING (Critical)

**Friction Summary:** Users experience login issues and duplicate account problems during the onboarding process.

**Flow Sequence:** Login Screen → Account Creation/Verification

**Highest Friction Screen:** Login Screen

| Screen | Pain Points | Severity | Description | Recommendation |
|--------|------------|----------|-------------|----------------|
| Login Screen | Login Issues | 5/5 | Users are unable to log in due to technical difficulties. | Implement a more robust login system with multiple authentication methods. |
| Account Creation/Verification | Duplicate Account Issues | 4/5 | Users encounter issues when trying to create or verify their accounts. | Improve account creation and verification processes to prevent duplicate accounts. |
