# Maya Branding Guidelines

## 1. Brand Identity
*   **Name:** Maya (formerly PayMaya)
*   **Tagline/Concept:** "Everything and a bank"
*   **Core Theme:** Gen Z Fintech, Digital-First, High Contrast, "Sleek but cheerful"

## 2. Color Palette
*   **Primary Color:**
    *   **Maya Green:** `#75EEA5` (Main Action, Buttons, Highlights)
*   **Secondary/Accent Colors:**
    *   **Maya Purple:** (Use a standard brand purple, e.g., `#6F42C1` or similar pending exact hex, but used in gradients with Green)
    *   **Dark Navy:** `#112432` (Backgrounds, Dark Mode base)
*   **Gradients:**
    *   **Purple-Green Gradient:** Used for key visuals like Maya QR.
*   **Backgrounds:**
    *   Strong preference for **Dark Mode** / Darker shades for the app interface.

## 3. Typography
*   **Headline Font:** **Tuka** (Custom typeface by Jo Malinis)
    *   *Characteristics:* Bold, quirky, features "ink traps in the shape of a bird's beak".
    *   *Usage:* Headlines, sub-headlines, short text, logos.
*   **Body Font:** **Cerebri Sans Pro** (by Hanken Design Co.)
    *   *Characteristics:* Clean, geometric sans-serif.
    *   *Usage:* Long-form text, UI copy, where readability is paramount.

## 4. Visual Language & UI
*   **Iconography & Imagery:**
    *   **3D Design:** Heavy use of 3D illustrations, motion graphics, and textures.
    *   **"Money in Motion":** Visuals inspired by physical cash and movement.
    *   **The "m" Logo:** Modeled after a monoline rendition of a maya bird.
*   **UI Components:**
    *   **Rounded Corners:** Heavy importance on rounded corners for buttons, cards, and containers.
    *   **High Contrast:** Bright vivid colors (Green/Purple) against dark backgrounds.

## 5. Tone of Voice
*   **Personality:** Concise, helpful, slightly informal ("friendly fintech").
*   **Approach:** Not stiff banking jargon. Empowering and modern.

## 6. Execution Notes for Designers
*   **Typography Distinction:** strictly use **Tuka** for "loud" text and **Cerebri Sans** for "quiet" text.
*   **Gradients:** Use the Purple-Green gradient to denote "Business-to-Customer" or special features, separating it from the pure Green of standard actions.
*   **3D Elements:** Where possible, use 3D rendered icons instead of flat 2D icons for major navigation or hero sections.
