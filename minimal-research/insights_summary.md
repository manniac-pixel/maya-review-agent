## Top 3 Competitive Gaps vs GCash

### 1. reliability
- **GCash Strength:** stable and reliable service
- **Maya Weakness:** frequent login issues, account freezes, and unauthorized transactions
- **Design Opportunity:** Implement a more robust error handling system to prevent frequent crashes and provide clear instructions for troubleshooting

### 2. customer support
- **GCash Strength:** responsive customer service team
- **Maya Weakness:** poor customer support, unhelpful AI-powered chatbots, and long wait times for human assistance
- **Design Opportunity:** Invest in human customer support agents who can provide personalized help and resolve issues efficiently

### 3. user experience
- **GCash Strength:** intuitive interface and easy account management
- **Maya Weakness:** complex and frustrating upgrade process, slow loading times, and frequent app crashes
- **Design Opportunity:** Simplify the onboarding process and improve app performance by optimizing code and reducing unnecessary features
